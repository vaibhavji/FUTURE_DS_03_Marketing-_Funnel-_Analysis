# Marketing Campaign Conversion Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Bank Marketing dataset to understand customer conversion performance and evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.

The objective is to identify conversion patterns, compare contact channels, analyze monthly campaign performance, and generate actionable business recommendations using Python.

**Internship Task:** Task 3 — Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis  
**Dataset:** Bank Marketing Dataset (UCI Machine Learning Repository)  
**Tools:** Google Colab, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Objectives

- Clean and prepare marketing campaign data.
- Calculate the overall customer conversion rate.
- Analyze successful and unsuccessful campaign outcomes.
- Compare conversion rates across contact channels.
- Evaluate monthly conversion performance.
- Analyze conversion patterns by campaign contact frequency.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to improve marketing effectiveness.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains records of a bank's direct marketing campaigns. The target variable `y` indicates whether a customer subscribed to a term deposit.

Important columns used in the analysis:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `age` | Customer's age |
| `job` | Customer's occupation |
| `education` | Education level |
| `contact` | Contact communication method |
| `month` | Month of the last contact |
| `campaign` | Number of contacts during the current campaign |
| `poutcome` | Outcome of the previous campaign |
| `y` | Subscription outcome (`yes` or `no`) |

The `bank-full.csv` file is used for the main analysis.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading
Loaded the Bank Marketing CSV dataset into Google Colab using Pandas.

### 2. Data Cleaning
- Checked duplicate records.
- Removed duplicate rows.
- Cleaned column names and text values.
- Checked missing values.
- Inspected dataset structure and data types.

### 3. Conversion Analysis
Calculated:
- Total campaign contacts
- Successful conversions
- Unsuccessful conversions
- Overall conversion rate
- Non-conversion rate

**Conversion Rate Formula:**

Conversion Rate (%) = (Successful Conversions / Total Campaign Contacts) × 100

### 4. Contact Channel Analysis
Compared conversion rates across available contact methods to understand differences in observed performance.

### 5. Monthly Performance Analysis
Compared conversion rates across months to identify periods with relatively higher or lower conversion rates.

### 6. Campaign Frequency Analysis
Analyzed conversion rates by the number of contacts made during the current campaign.

### 7. Previous Campaign Analysis
Compared current conversion outcomes across previous campaign results.

### 8. Visualization and Dashboard
Created charts to communicate conversion outcomes, contact channel performance, monthly trends, and campaign contact frequency.

## Business Insights

The analysis is designed to help answer the following questions:

- Which contact methods have higher observed conversion rates?
- Which months show relatively stronger conversion performance?
- How does conversion rate vary with campaign contact frequency?
- Are previous campaign outcomes associated with current conversion results?
- Which areas deserve further marketing optimization and testing?

## Business Recommendations

1. **Optimize contact channels:** Investigate the reasons behind differences in conversion rates and test improvements to targeting and messaging.
2. **Improve campaign timing:** Explore monthly conversion differences and test alternative campaign schedules.
3. **Manage contact frequency:** Evaluate repeated contacts and test whether an appropriate contact limit improves efficiency.
4. **Use previous campaign outcomes:** Investigate whether previous campaign success can help prioritize future outreach.
5. **Track marketing costs:** Add campaign expenditure data to calculate cost per conversion and return on marketing investment.
6. **Monitor performance regularly:** Track conversion rates over time to identify changes and evaluate campaign improvements.

## Limitations

- The dataset records campaign contacts and subscription outcomes, not website visitors or a complete visitors-to-leads funnel.
- Conversion in this project means subscription to a term deposit.
- The analysis identifies associations and descriptive patterns; it does not establish causation.
- Marketing spend and revenue are not included in the selected dataset, so ROI cannot be calculated directly.
- Campaign duration and contact frequency should be interpreted carefully when making recommendations.
